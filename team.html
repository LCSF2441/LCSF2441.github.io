About Our Team

Carry-on is comprised of experts and hard-workers who are dedicated to providing the best possible products in the most efficient manner so customers have the perfect experience on the their travels.

Carry-on was founded in 2003 by Francis H. Case and first operated out of his garage in Sarnia, Ontario. Two years later he obtained his first warehouse, and since then the team has grown and improved.

Currently our team is comprised of over 70 talented individuals dedicated to creating the best possible service for our customers. Keep scrolling to see some of our team members.

Facts

  • Carry-on now has three warehouses located in Sarnia, London, and Windsor.
  • Carry-on is the most popular provider of luggage in southern Ontario
  • Carry-on has a large customer support team with over 10 members dedicated to assisting you.
